ACCOUNT DESK - 5 MINUTE PRESENTATION SCRIPT
(read from phone, show CRM on laptop)

LINK: https://account-desk-crm.onrender.com
Open it 1 minute early. Wait for the green
"MCP live" pill at the top.

KEYS: x = Cockpit   p = Portfolio
      1 = Eastern Counties
      a = Account plan   y = Playbook
      l = Change log   c = AI Copilot
      Esc = close

==================================
1. OPENING  (0:00 - 0:30)
==================================
SHOW: Cockpit  -> press x

SAY:
"Hi everyone. This is Account Desk,
the CRM I built for the accounts
I took over from Sam.

I got a police alliance and an
industrial group. Two more accounts
arrived during the build.

On paper all of them looked fine.
This tool shows what is really
going on, and what to do next.

This is cockpit mode: health of
each account, renewal countdowns,
next actions on the clock, red
risks, and a live feed of data
coming in."

DO: press Esc

==================================
2. PORTFOLIO  (0:30 - 1:00)
==================================
SHOW: Portfolio  -> press p

SAY:
"This is the Monday morning view.

Each account shows its health
score, renewal value and days left.

Two lines on each card:
what the account sheet SAYS,
and what the data SHOWS.

Example: the police alliance says
the rollout is going well. The data
says only 17 of 35 docks really fly.

Below is this week's priority list
for all accounts."

==================================
3. FIVE QUESTIONS  (1:00 - 2:30)
==================================
SHOW: Eastern Counties -> press 1

SAY:
"Every account answers the same
five questions a manager asks."

DO: click  1 . Book
SAY:
"ONE - What is it worth?
Renewal is 350,000 pounds, backed
by the contract.
But 180,000 of it pays for docks
that hardly fly.
Things like the 300-dock tender are
only claims, so I keep them separate
and never add them to the total."

DO: click  2 . Power
SAY:
"TWO - Who holds the power?
For each deal: who decides, who
pays, who uses it, who can block.
Red boxes = people we never met.
Our main contact Rhys is leaving in
January. That is a risk."

DO: click  3 . First moves
SAY:
"THREE - What do I do first?
Ranked actions, each with an owner,
a due date and a ready draft message.
Number one: reply to Inspector Karen
before her 5th December event."

DO: click  4 . About to go wrong
SAY:
"FOUR - What can go wrong?
Every risk has a colour and the
date it turns bad."

DO: click  5 . Unknowns
SAY:
"FIVE - What we still don't know,
and who can tell us.
I show gaps honestly, no guessing."

==================================
4. NUMBERS HAVE SOURCES (2:30 - 3:00)
==================================
SHOW: still Eastern Counties, tab 1

DO: click the small "contract.txt"
    tag under 350,000

SAY:
"Nothing here is typed by hand.
Every number has a small tag.
Click it and you see the original
record. This is the contract:
350,000 a year for 35 docks."

DO: press Esc

==================================
5. IT STAYS LIVE  (3:00 - 3:45)
==================================
DO: click the green "MCP live" pill
SAY:
"The CRM pulls data by itself from
the game every minute through the
MCP connection."

DO: press Esc, then press l
    (Change log)
SAY:
"When new data came in this
afternoon, it updated with no code
change.
Example: 3 busy docks at Aldermoor
suddenly stopped flying.
The CRM caught it, raised a red risk
and made it the number one action."

DO: point at Brindle Harbour and
    Kestrel in the left menu
SAY:
"Two brand new accounts, Brindle
Harbour and Kestrel, also appeared
on their own."

==================================
6. THE PLAN  (3:45 - 4:30)
==================================
SHOW: Account plan -> press a
DO: scroll down to "Unlock map"

SAY:
"This is the plan I would present.
Each line shows what unlocks what.

Tarn Valley: their docks never flew
because they are waiting for a
document.
Deliver the document -> they fly ->
we prove value -> THEN we ask about
more docks or a stadium.

Fix first. Prove second.
Grow third."

SHOW: Playbook -> press y
SAY:
"This is the Playbook: mistakes we
made and will not repeat. For
example, never pitch more docks
while the current ones sit idle.
It is also a checklist for the next
account we onboard."

==================================
7. AI COPILOT + CLOSE (4:30 - 5:00)
==================================
SHOW: AI Copilot -> press c
DO: click "What should I do first
    on Monday, and why?"

SAY:
"You can also ask questions in plain
English. The AI answers only from
this data and shows a source for
every point."

CLOSE:
"So: every number has a source,
actions are ranked with owners and
dates, gaps are honest, and it
stays live.
That is Account Desk.
Happy to take questions."

==================================
IF THEY ASK
==================================
Q: Where does the data come from?
A: "From the game through its MCP
   connection. My server calls it
   once a minute."

Q: How are priorities decided?
A: "Money at stake, how likely it
   works, and how soon it is due."

Q: Can it handle a new account?
A: "Yes. Two new accounts arrived
   during the build and showed up
   automatically."

Q: Is the AI making things up?
A: "No. It can only use this data,
   and the app checks every source
   it cites."
